Months 3 & 4

A Real 2-Month Internship

Not an optional task list. You work on live client or in-house brand accounts under mentor supervision, with weekly deliverables and structured feedback.

What you hand over

  • ✓Weekly channel-wise performance reports, reviewed by your mentor
  • ✓One owned live campaign or automation shipped to production
  • ✓A final presentation to IDM faculty and a client stakeholder
  • ✓An internship completion letter and performance appraisal

The timeline

How the Career Track Runs

  1. 01

    Months 1–2 · Build the evidence

    Twelve modules, each ending in a live exercise on a real or simulated brand. Every result is documented as it happens, because reconstructing numbers later never works.

  2. 02

    Module 12 · Package it

    The capstone pulls strategy, content, ads, automation and analytics into one campaign, presented to a review panel. Resume, LinkedIn and portfolio get built alongside it.

  3. 03

    Months 3–4 · Internship

    Two months on live client or in-house brand accounts under mentor supervision, with weekly deliverables and a performance appraisal.

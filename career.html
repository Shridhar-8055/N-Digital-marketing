How the Career Track Runs

    Weeks 1–7 · Build the evidence

    Campaign work becomes case studies. Resume and LinkedIn get rebuilt around outcomes. Your portfolio goes live as a real, linkable thing.

  3. 03

    Week 14 · Pick your path

This notice explains what we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

When you submit the application form, the brochure form or a callback request, we receive the name, phone number, email address, current status and city you type in, along with which form you used and when. Nothing else about you is collected by those forms.

Why we collect it

To contact you about the programme you enquired about: to send the syllabus, arrange a call, confirm an interview slot and answer questions. We do not sell your details or pass them to unrelated third parties.

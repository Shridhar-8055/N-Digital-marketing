How you are graded

There Is No Certificate Without the Work

Completion requires at least 80% attendance in the practical phase, a successful capstone submission, and a satisfactory internship mentor sign-off.

    The capstone

    In the final five days you pick a real or simulated brand and build one complete AI-powered campaign: strategy and ICP, an AI-assisted content calendar, a live ad set plus an organic sprint, one working automation or AI agent, and a GA4 dashboard with KPIs and attribution — then present it to an internal review panel.
